Synthetic firm policy for a payments support queue. A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction about the firm's own service, staff, advice, charges, systems, or handling that alleges loss, distress, or material inconvenience. A customer need not use the word complaint, ask for an investigation, or be correct. A refund does not erase dissatisfaction about prior harm. Repeat contact, needing support, asking about procedures, or a problem caused solely by a merchant or the customer is insufficient without dissatisfaction attributable to the firm. A referral or an agent's reply does not by itself record a complaint. Only an actual register entry counts as logged.
